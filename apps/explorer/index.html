About the data and methods

Search satellite catalogue metadata

Search published observations by coordinate, date and mission. The explorer queries Earth Search and Microsoft Planetary Computer and preserves each catalogue's provenance. Results describe available assets and access conditions; they do not promise free imagery or complete historical coverage.

Published observations and acquisition plans

Historical results are published catalogue records. Upcoming ESA acquisitions are planned datatakes, not images that already exist or guaranteed future captures. Partial searches and missing metadata remain explicitly labelled. Cloud percentages describe the scene, not cloud conditions inside a selected area.

Sources, access and limitations

Dates are shown in UTC. Footprints and area coverage are approximate. Preview access is checked when opened, and each provider's imagery terms still apply. The map, interactive search and scene previews require JavaScript; this overview and the form labels remain available without it.
